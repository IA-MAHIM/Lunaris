# LUNARIS — 30-second NASA global cut

The Moon's south pole creates difficult sunlight and Earth-communication windows. We are Team CodexGalaxy from IUBAT, Bangladesh, and this is LUNARIS: our interactive Lunar Landing Site Navigator. Choose a landing coordinate and date, explore Sun and Earth positions, watch visibility windows change, and compare alternate mission scenarios. A simple optimizer and power model make the trade-offs easier to understand. Our calculations are clearly labeled as estimates unless NASA JPL Horizons data has been imported. LUNARIS: Plan. Optimize. Simulate.
